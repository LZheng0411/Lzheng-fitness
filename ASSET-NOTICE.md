# Asset notice

The workbench ships with project-owned PNG and MP4 background files under `skills/lzheng-fitness-workbench-builder/assets/backgrounds/`.

The repository maintainer has authorized their inclusion in this package and in workbenches generated by its initialization script. The MP4 motion background always has a local PNG fallback. Do not replace these files with third-party assets unless you hold the corresponding distribution rights. The files contain no personal training data.

End users may replace generated-workbench backgrounds with `Replace-FitnessWorkbenchBackground.py`. They are responsible for the usage and distribution rights of supplied images and videos; custom user assets must not be committed to the public repository without explicit authorization.
